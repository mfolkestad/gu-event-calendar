Reorienting Automation, reframing AI with Daniela Rosner and Cristina Zaga

Research

On September 18, 2026, Business and Design Lab will open the 2026-2027 seminar series with a conversation on robots, algorithms and possibilities of reorientation and reframing with Daniela Rosner and Cristina Zaga. In two presentations and following discussions, the seminar asks how we might understand AI, automation and robotics differently when we attend to the materials, labor, infrastructures, and relations on which they depend.
